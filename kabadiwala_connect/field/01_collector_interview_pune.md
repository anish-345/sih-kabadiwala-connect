# Field Interview 01: Informal Scrap Collector (Pune)

**Date**: 28 September 2026, 11:30 AM IST  
**Location**: Nana Peth Scrap Market / Timber Market Lane, Pune, Maharashtra  
**Participant**: Ramesh Shinde (42 years old)  
**Profile**: Itinerant scrap collector (kabadiwala) with pedal tricycle cart; 18 years in informal recycling.  
**Language**: Marathi (primary), colloquial Hindi.  
**Device**: Realme C11 (2GB RAM, Android 11 Go Edition, cracked screen, 4G prepaid with spotty data).  

---

## 1. Current Operations & Material Flow
- **Collection Pattern**: Travels 12–15 km daily through residential housing societies (Kothrud, Swargate, Camp) calling out for "bhangar/raddi".
- **E-Waste Volumes**: Collects ~35–50 kg of mixed electronics weekly (old TVs, microwave ovens, desktop motherboards, mobile chargers, discarded power supplies).
- **Current Selling Point**: Sells directly to a local aggregator ("Bada Seth") situated in Nana Peth.
- **Selling Rate Realized**:
  - Desktop Motherboards (Mid PCB): Sold at **₹80 to ₹100 per kg**.
  - Insulated Copper Wires: Sold at **₹260 to ₹300 per kg** (or burns insulation in open fields at night to sell bare copper wire for ₹550/kg, releasing toxic dioxins).
  - CRT Monitors: Aggregator pays flat **₹50 per piece** (regardless of 15 kg weight, effectively ~₹3.3/kg).
  - Lithium Phone Batteries: Thrown in with mixed scrap or discarded in municipal bins because local scrap shop refuses them due to fire risk.

---

## 2. Key Pain Points Uncovered
1. **Aggregator Price Deduction & Cheating**:
   > *"Seth cuts 20% weight citing 'moisture and plastic weight'. We have no way to dispute it. Whatever rate he writes on the slip, we have to take."*
2. **Ignorance of Government EPR Schemes & Formal Gates**:
   - Ramesh had zero awareness of CPCB EPR certificates or that formal recyclers in MIDC Bhosari buy motherboards at **₹160 to ₹190/kg**.
   - Believes formal factories only deal in "truckloads (10 tons) with GST invoices".
3. **Cash Dependency**:
   - Needs spot cash by 6:00 PM every evening to purchase daily rations and cart maintenance. Cannot wait 7 days for NEFT/RTGS bank transfers.
4. **App Usability Constraints**:
   - Cannot read long English or Marathi sentences. Navigates by colors, icons, and voice cues.
   - Frequent loss of cellular connectivity in narrow lanes of Old Pune (Nana Peth, Bhavani Peth).

---

## 3. Platform Validation Feedback
- **Spoken Rates**: When shown a mock screen that spoke the Hindi rate aloud ("आज कॉम्प्युटर मदरबोर्ड का भाव ₹190 प्रति किलो है"), Ramesh listened twice and smiled: *"यह सही है, आवाज़ से पता चल जाता है।"*
- **Offline Mode**: Tested creating a draft lot without internet: approved because network drops frequently during rounds.
- **Immediate Cash Voucher**: Enthusiastic about authorized recyclers offering physical cash at the gate upon QR scan: *"अगर तुरंत नकद मिले और भाव ₹50 ज़्यादा हो, तो हम Bhosari पिकअप पॉइंट पर ही माल देंगे।"*
