# Field Interview 02: Scrap Sorter & Micro-Aggregator (Pimpri-Chinchwad)

**Date**: 28 September 2026, 3:15 PM IST  
**Location**: Kudale Patil Scrap Cluster, Chikhali Road, Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC), Maharashtra  
**Participant**: Sushila Bai Jadhav (38 years old)  
**Profile**: Micro-aggregator and material sorter; manages a 200 sq.ft covered scrap shed with her brother; purchases from 8–10 door-to-door ragpickers.  
**Language**: Marathi (fluent), Hindi (basic).  
**Device**: Redmi 9A (32GB storage, Android 10, Jio 4G connection).  

---

## 1. Sorting & Aggregation Operations
- **Throughput**: Processes approximately 250–400 kg of e-waste monthly, collected from industrial fabrication units and residential complexes in Akurdi and Nigdi.
- **Manual Dismantling**:
  - Uses screwdrivers and hammers to crack open desktop casings, washing machine timers, and ceiling fan stators.
  - Separates printed circuit boards into three cardboard boxes: "green boards with chips", "brown single-sided boards", and "cables/wires".
- **Safety Hazards Observed**:
  - Suffered cuts from broken CRT glass funnel corners.
  - Experienced an accidental thermal runaway smoke flare-up two months ago when puncturing a swollen lithium-ion pouch battery with pliers.
  - Burns electrical cable bundles on open corrugated iron sheets when manual stripping takes too much time.

---

## 2. Economics & Revenue Gap
- **Selling Price to Large Intermediary Traders (Dhamnod/Bhiwandi)**:
  - High-grade telecom boards: Sold at ₹280/kg (trader sells to smelters at >₹400/kg).
  - Copper motor windings: Sold at ₹480/kg.
  - Lead-acid inverter batteries: ₹72/kg.
- **Transportation & Friction**:
  - Hires a small tempo (Piaggio Ape) costing ₹600 per trip to transport 200 kg to wholesale traders in Bhosari.
  - Traders routinely deduct 10–15% citing "contamination / dirt / plastic remnants".

---

## 3. Platform Requirement Insights
1. **Pictorial Safety Warnings are Vital**:
   - When Sushila saw the hazardous material warning (warning sign with red border and fire icon for batteries, plus spoken Marathi warning: *"बॅटरी कापू नका, आग लागण्याचा धोका आहे"*), she confirmed that pictorial guides will prevent serious burn injuries among women sorters.
2. **Cluster Pickup Aggregation**:
   - If an authorized recycler offers free scheduled tempo pickup for lots above 25 kg, she saves ₹600 freight costs per trip, directly increasing net profit margins by 18%.
3. **Traceable Cash Receipts (EPR Verification)**:
   - *"अगर रीसायकलर हमें सरकारी बिल/रसीद दे जिसमें हमारा नाम और वज़न लिखा हो, तो पुलिस या नगर निगम वाले हमें परेशान नहीं कर सकते।"* (A formal digital handover receipt protects informal workers from harassment by local authorities).
