## 2. Why the Recycler Willingly Pays

Authorized recyclers face heavy penalties from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) if they fail to meet mandatory annual EPR targets (E-Waste Rules 2022).
- **CPCB Non-Compliance Penalty / Market Certificate Cost**: ~₹65 to ₹85 per kg.
- **Kabadiwala Connect Verification Cost**: Recycler pays only **₹20/kg EPR incentive + 2% platform convenience fee (₹3.80/kg)**.
- **Recycler Net Savings**:
  $$ \text{Savings per kg} = ₹75 \text{ (Market Penalty)} - (₹20 + ₹3.80) = ₹51.20 \text{ saved per kg} $$
- In addition, the recycler receives **100% verifiable, tamper-evident audit trails** with GPS stamps, photos, and digital lot IDs that pass MoEFCC audit checks on the first attempt.

## 3. Platform Sustainability Formula

$$\text{Platform Revenue} = \text{Recycler Platform Fee (2\% of lot value or ₹1.5/kg)} \times \text{Handover Volume}$$

$$\text{Monthly Cost} = \text{Local Cloud Hosting (₹1,800)} + \text{SMS/WhatsApp Gateway for QR slips (₹0.15/msg)} + \text{Field Lead Agent (₹12,000)}$$

- **Break-Even Volume**:
  $$\text{At ₹1.50/kg, platform breaks even at } \frac{₹14,000}{₹1.50} = 9.3 \text{ tons of e-waste/month (approx. 25 active collectors).}$$
- **Zero Tax on Informal Labor**: The economic model charges the formal compliance buyer, not the subsistence collector.
